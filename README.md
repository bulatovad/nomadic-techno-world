# 🪐 Nomadic Techno World

> **An alternative futuristic universe where high technology meets a traditional nomadic lifestyle.**

---

## 📌 About the Concept

**Nomadic Techno World** is a visual worldbuilding and design fiction project exploring the concept of an advanced technological nomadic civilization. 

In this world, humanity has preserved harmony with nature and freedom of movement by utilizing autonomous mobile living units, robotic platforms, solar power, and satellite navigation.

---

## 🏗️ Catalog of Objects & Technologies

### 🏠 1. Mobile Dwellings
* **Walking House (NOMAD-01)** — An autonomous mobile dwelling created as a technological continuation of the traditional yurt.
  * **Energy:** Solar panels, wind turbines, waste recovery.
  * **Water Supply:** Rain and snow collection, filtration systems.
  * **Navigation & Communication:** Autonomous pathfinding, satellite internet connection.

### 🛰️ 2. Infrastructure & Satellites
* **Satellite Network:** Provides global positioning, telemetry, and communication links for mobile units.
* **Central Domes & Hubs:** Stationary and semi-stationary technological and research centers.

### 🤖 3. Robotics & Drones
* Autonomous drones and mobile units used for resource extraction, environmental monitoring, and infrastructure maintenance.
